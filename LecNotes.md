##### Space Syntax
- Comes from the world of architecture, who first published on it in the mid 80s, then archaeologists adopted it in the 90s. Associated with the work of Hiller and Hanson aiming to 'socialize' the post war people of France. 
- Building as a living space, relationships are constructed through spatial design. Theory of Pedagogy in built space.
- Depth, Choice, Integration.
- Space Syntax was incredibly popular, and during this streak of popularity software was developed to allow the easy translation of site plans in digital formats. This software now has become largely obsolete, and replaced by small hand written diagrams.
- Space Syntax is a set of theoretical statements that allow us to derive a methods that then lets us ground an interpretation using the data we have. This technique is a way of seeing small clusters in small spaces in the same buildings or cities.

##### Machine Learning vs 'AI' 
- Machine learning is where we look for patterns by using a Neural Network, Language models are a subsect of Machine Learning.
- Two types of Machine learning: Supervised: we know the categories, we just want to be better at finding these categories. Depends on having good example categories. Looks for patterns when we know the categories. Unsupervised: Just says, "we dont have the lables or categories. Look at this data and see if it clusters somehow". Learns patterns on its own. Works better with clean data, archaeological data isn't always as tidy.
- The question of "What was used to train the Machine Model?" is often a more important question than "How the machine has categorized?"

##### Vesuvius Challenge: 
- Mass collection of Papyri found through tunnelling. Collecting these pieces was easy, reading them was another issue. Through CAT scanning, and by slicing, you could vitrually unwrap them to see the length of the scroll, and then through the chemical difference between the Papyri and ink, we could read the virtually unfurled sheets.
- Brickette (Papyri) is unfurled > a trained network uses the difference between what it knows vs whats present, then adjusts the weight until data presents itself.
- Taphonomy = study of processes that change object in the earth. A Taphonomic classifier can be used to differentiate human remains through processes they've exhibited.
- Adversarial networks = training of one network and training of another one to fool the other.

##### Machine Translation 
- If you can recognize images in neural networks, you can recognize text way easier.
- Machine Translators figured out a neural network that would respond to english texts used to predict missing words, and it would learn to associate one and the other.
- One of the side affects of this was that if you allowed it to keep predicting missing words it would just keep generating text.
- Thus was born generative AI out of this Neural Network pathway. "LANGUAGE MODELS" = Machine Translations. Reinforcment learning.
